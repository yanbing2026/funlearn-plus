FAQ

What ages is this for?

Primarily ages 3–6 (preschool through kindergarten). Every resource notes the recommended age and how to play.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage your subscription from "My Account" — it stays active until the end of the current billing period, then simply doesn't renew.

How do the printables work?

Members download PDFs directly and print at home or at a print shop. We recommend cardstock (65 lb+) for flashcards.

Is payment secure?

Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. Sign-in is passwordless via an email link.
